# Paper type: review

Survey or meta-analysis.

- Section 02 becomes the review's organizing question and taxonomy.
- Section 06-08 shrink: only the systems/formulas the review itself
  synthesizes (e.g., a property-prediction equation across studies).
- Section 09 becomes synthesis transparency: how studies were selected,
  compared, and weighted; note if the method is unstated.
- Section 10 maps what characterization evidence the review relies on
  across the surveyed corpus.
- Section 13 critiques coverage and bias (database, timeframe, language,
 venue bias) from the paper's own stated scope.
- Section 15 is central for reviews: connect the taxonomy to adjacent
  fields and open problems.
