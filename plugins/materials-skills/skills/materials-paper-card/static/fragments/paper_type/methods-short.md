# Paper type: methods-short

Method note, communication, or short paper.

- Sections 06-08 focus tightly on the method novelty: what it measures,
  what it improves over, its validated range.
- Section 09 scales down: validation experiments only, with the paper's
  own validation criteria quoted.
- Section 11 states applicability boundaries explicitly — method notes
  often over-generalize; keep the tested materials and conditions.
- Section 14 emphasizes the method's transferability checklist.
