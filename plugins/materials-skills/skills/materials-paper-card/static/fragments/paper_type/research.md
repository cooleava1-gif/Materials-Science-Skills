# Paper type: research

Experimental or computational study with new claims.

- Section 06 carries the full material system: composition/mix design,
  processing route, key parameters, standards.
- Section 09 is the core: every central claim mapped to experiments,
  baselines, replicates, and reported effect sizes.
- Section 10 reads the characterization chain as the mechanism argument
  (phase identification → microstructure → property link).
- Section 11 must state the tested envelope (composition range, curing
  regime, load cases) — conclusions outside it are boundaries, not claims.
- Reviewer-style methodological critique stays out of Section 13 unless
  evidence-tied (invented critique is forbidden).
