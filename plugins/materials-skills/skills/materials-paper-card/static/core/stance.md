# Operating Stance

- The card serves one reader goal: understand and reuse the paper's
  knowledge honestly. Density beats completeness; an honest `Not assessable`
  beats a plausible guess.
- Separate what the paper **reports**, what it **claims**, and what the
  evidence **supports** — Sections 09-11 exist to keep those apart.
- Materials specifics are first-class: material system, mix design or
  processing route, characterization chain, and structure-property links get
  their own sections (06, 10), not a footnote.
- Match the user's language for headings and prose; keep technical terms,
  phase names, and formulas canonical.
- Reuse before re-deriving: an existing reader package is the preferred
  evidence base; do not re-extract or re-summarize what it already carries.
