# Evidence and Provenance Reference

How analytical statements in Sections 09-13 earn their place.

## Statement classes

| Class | Rule | Marker |
|---|---|---|
| Reported | directly stated by the paper | cited locator; no marker needed |
| Analytic | your reading of the paper's logic | must cite the locators it follows from |
| Comparative | positions the paper against others | external context mode required |
| Speculative | hypothesis beyond the paper | allowed only in Section 16, gated |

## What counts as evidence in a materials paper

- Quantitative results: strengths, moduli, durability metrics, with
  replicates and conditions as reported.
- Characterization: XRD phase lists, FTIR band assignments, SEM/TEM
  morphology descriptions, TG mass-loss steps, EDS compositions — each
  supports only its own inference level (identification ≠ mechanism).
- Standards invoked: ASTM / ISO / GB methods define measurement validity;
  note when a claimed standard compliance is only implied.
- Statistics as reported: the card reports the paper's tests and n without
  re-analysis (statistical critique belongs to `materials-statistics` via
  handoff, not inline re-computation).

## Prohibited moves

- Presenting the paper's related-work narrative as independently verified
  history (mark it as the paper's account).
- Using one characterization to confirm an unrelated mechanism (e.g., SEM
  morphology "confirming" a chemical pathway).
- Quietly filling a missing-control gap with "presumably".
- External claims without recording the context mode.

## External verification scope

Allowed only for Section 01 (bibliographic), Section 15 (connections), and
explicit novelty checks. Record which sources were consulted and when; the
card never silently blends external facts with paper-internal evidence.
