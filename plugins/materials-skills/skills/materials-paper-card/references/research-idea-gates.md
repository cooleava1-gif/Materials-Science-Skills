# Research Idea Gates (Section 16)

Every idea in Section 16 must pass all four gates, in writing:

1. **Gap gate** — names the specific evidence gap or untested boundary the
   idea attacks (tied to Sections 09-12), not a generic "future work".
2. **Testability gate** — a one-paragraph experiment sketch: variables,
   material system, key measurements, and what result would falsify the
   idea. Ideas needing new instruments or multi-year programmes say so.
3. **Boundary gate** — respects the paper's tested envelope: no idea
   silently extrapolates composition, curing, or scale.
4. **Reuse gate** — states which of the paper's methods/data the idea
   reuses (cheap wins first); dataset reuse routes to `materials-data`.

Formatting: each idea is one numbered block:

```markdown
### Idea N: <one line>
- Gap: <from Sections 09-12>
- Sketch: <variables, system, measurements, falsifier>
- Boundary: <envelope respected / explicitly extended>
- Reuse: <methods/data reused>
```

Three to five ideas is the sweet spot; quality gates beat quantity.
Hand the chosen idea to `materials-research` for routing into a full plan
(reading → citation → DOE → writing), not into an uncontrolled experiment.
